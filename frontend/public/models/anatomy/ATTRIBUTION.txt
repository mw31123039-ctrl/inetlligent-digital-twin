Selected original meshes; welded duplicate position vertices and recomputed smooth normals; source Z-up to Y-up with anterior -Y mapped to +Z; uniform regional centering/scaling; illustrative PBR colors and subtle vertex variation; exported GLB. No polygon decimation or patient-specific reconstruction.
Detailed source identifiers, hashes and transformations are recorded in manifest.json.
These reference anatomical assets are educational and are not patient scans or clinically validated by this application.
