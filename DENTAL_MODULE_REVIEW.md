# Dental and orthodontic research workspace

Implemented 5 November 2026. Open `http://127.0.0.1:5174/#dental`, `/#orthodontics`, or `/#dental-data` after the portable build. The sidebar's **Dental studio**, and the **Dental pain** / **Braces & bite** area buttons, open this workspace. These two area buttons replace the Arms and Legs launchers. Legacy limb IDs, records, assets and patient-twin observations remain readable.

## What works

### Orthodontic screening annotation and demonstration added 8 November 2026

Open **Cases & practice → Orthodontic screening**. Existing case photos can be used, or standard dental photos can be attached in the screening view. The existing simulator, 26-topic teaching library, student practice and local crown learner remain available. The new 41-condition taxonomy is separate from the older teaching-example IDs so existing exports retain their meaning.

`contracts/dental/screening-taxonomy.v1.json` preserves all 41 supplied condition names/definitions, four categories, descriptive broader/related links, photographic/clinical evidence scopes, view guidance, limitations, four separate dental classifications and 14 verified professional source links. IDs are stable snake_case. **No orthodontist has reviewed this implementation; global definition review is pending.** In-app review fields are explicitly self-attested and do not verify professional credentials, clinical validity or treatment need.

Every condition starts as `not_assessable`, with explicit `present`, `absent`, `uncertain` and `not_assessable` states. Multiple labels may coexist; broader/related labels are never filled automatically. The record includes coded subject/visit IDs, one patient partition, recorded or unknown age, primary/mixed/permanent/unknown dentition, normal appearance, braces, image-set scope and per-image view, quality, quality reasons, biting-together status and patient-side orientation. Permanent and primary FDI IDs are manual, optional and checked for consistency; unknown identity stays unknown. Overjet and overbite are distinct labels. No distances, severity grades or patient confidence scores are invented.

The summary describes **reviewer-entered** observations and their evidence basis, not AI detections. Photographic findings require selected, confirmed, usable supporting views; bite observations also require a recorded biting-together state. Clinical-record-only concepts are withheld from photographic results. Combined photo/record concepts may have visible appearances but need records for established research labels. Missing teeth in an image never establish agenesis, impaction or delayed eruption. Insufficient/out-of-scope or unconfirmed inputs do not become accepted photographic findings. Empty annotations are not a negative screen. Explicit reviewer-entered normal appearance carries the warning that treatment need and hidden conditions remain unassessed.

Molar relationships are recorded separately for right/left, canine relationships separately for right/left, and incisor relationships separately. Overall/incisor classifications support Class I, Class II division 1, Class II division 2 and Class III; molar/canine relationships use Class I/II/III. All remain clinician-entered and do not imply a skeletal diagnosis. General care options retain their existing educational benefits/tradeoffs; no patient-specific appliance, extraction, surgery, bracket, wire, force or timing plan is generated.

**Demonstration mode** uses separate prewritten synthetic examples, including crowding/rotation, spacing, open bite, normal-appearance wording, insufficient images and out-of-scope inputs. It does not inspect patient photos, change their labels or enter the research store. **No condition classifier is supplied.** A taxonomy or prompt does not train a vision classifier, and the existing crown-outline forest cannot classify these conditions.

Explicit local persistence uses owner-scoped `GET/POST /api/dental/screening/cases` and `GET/DELETE /api/dental/screening/cases/{hash}`, backed by the private Python service and the account's existing local teaching SQLite file. Saves require storage consent and recorded data rights. Metadata-only saves omit image pixels; including re-encoded JPEG pixels requires a separate visible choice. Source/prepared fingerprints bind supplied bytes. Lists return coded visit metadata, not photos; loading a saved visit is explicit. Records are schema-checked in PHP and Python and retain all 41 distinct labels. All visits/images with one patient code share a partition across screening and crown teaching; exact-photo reassignment across patients/partitions is rejected. Codes/hash checks cannot prove real-world patient independence; dataset curation must verify it. Deletion clears/vacuums the saved screening record and leaves the separate crown-training dataset unchanged. Local files are private workspace files, not encrypted storage.

Annotation JSON exports omit pixels. A separate research photo-bundle export requires recorded data rights, training permission, resolved evidence/location issues, and self-attested orthodontist definition/annotation review. Review is invalidated after clinical edits. Unreviewed drafts can still be saved for later professional review. None of these actions trains or validates a condition classifier.

**Per-condition performance** starts as not evaluated. An optional local browser evaluator accepts one screening record or an array of reviewed test records and an explicit `humantwin-screening-predictions-v1` model-output bundle. It requires model ID/version, a threshold-policy declaration, declared training/validation patients, matching visit/photo hashes and all 41 output states. It rejects patient leakage, duplicate/missing visits, unreviewed/contradictory truth, changed hashes and invalid probabilities. Only present/absent truth is eligible; uncertain/not-assessable truth is excluded. Sensitivity/recall, specificity and precision use covered binary predictions, with coverage and abstention counts shown separately. Calibration uses only actual supplied probabilities: Brier score and ten-bin expected calibration error. Missing denominators/scores stay unevaluated. Brier combines probability calibration and discrimination; ECE is a sample/bin-dependent estimate. Multiple visits per patient are correlated. Model provenance/review are declared rather than independently certified, and calculated metrics do not establish clinical suitability. See the primary [metric definitions](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/model_evaluation.html) and [calibration guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/calibration.html).

Validation includes taxonomy/state/evidence/location/evaluation unit checks, Python schema/persistence/account/split tests, PHP authentication/schema/permission/filtering/size-limit tests, and a synthetic browser workflow for save/load/delete, reviewed exports, evidence withholding, evaluation, injection escaping, mobile fit and preservation of the case workspace. No patient dataset or credentialed condition inference was evaluated. Specialist review, representative licensed/consented clinical data, condition-model training and external validation remain outstanding.

### Public-data tooth detector added 8 November 2026

An explicit local GPU training pipeline now uses the [DentalMate6v RGB tooth-box export](https://universe.roboflow.com/dentalmate6v/intraoral-tooth-numbering-fdi/dataset/1), CC BY 4.0, through a fingerprinted public mirror. It trains YOLO11n from pinned official base weights, collapsing numbering classes to visible-tooth localization. The export contains 2,541 augmented training images, 246 validation images and 120 test images. Source-image/exact-file overlaps are checked across those splits; patient separation and independent annotation review are **not verified**. Test metrics measure image-level boxes, not clinical accuracy or crown masks. The run report records actual results; no published model accuracy is copied into app status. No private app photos are used. Ultralytics software/base weights use AGPL-3.0.

The finished ONNX model is loaded offline by OpenCV from `DENTAL_PUBLIC_MODEL_DIR`, with SHA-256 verification. Boxes seed approximate per-image GrabCut boundaries; no supervised crown-mask model has been trained. The public baseline supplies drafts when the account has no valid personal model. Account-specific training still stays private and takes precedence. Inference makes no network calls, saves no photos, predicts no FDI identity or orthodontic conditions, and requires the existing review gate before movement. The app reports source/counts without inventing foreground Dice. Recent training progress is visible while the run is active. No weights/data are committed to Git. Training and deployment to this local workspace do not establish clinical suitability.

### Photo-matched simulator added 7 November 2026

Uploading a photograph in the case room now opens a separate **Photo-matched dental simulator**, above the explicitly generic teaching atlas. The original and mock-up render the exact locally attached photo. The mock-up clips reviewer-defined visible crown regions from that image and applies reviewer-controlled 2D translations and rotations. It never substitutes a stock tooth atlas for the uploaded photograph. Moving regions exposes a configurable flat fill; hidden oral tissue is not reconstructed. This is a geometric visual discussion tool, not photorealistic inpainting, biomechanical simulation, patient-specific 3D reconstruction or a prediction that those changes are possible.

- **Local detection and assistance:** authenticated, rate-limited `POST /api/dental/detect` uses a separately trained CPU OpenCV random forest through the private service. It learns background/interior/edge pixels from colour and texture features, then extracts rough contour candidates. Until a real model is trained, detection is unavailable. `POST /api/dental/segment` refines one human-drawn polygon using per-image GrabCut foreground/background models; no saved dental weights are needed. Assisted segmentation does not update the saved detector. Neither endpoint calls an external provider. No FDI identity, occlusion condition, severity, treatment target or appliance is inferred. Local models may miss/merge teeth, include non-tooth pixels or fail on unseen lighting/cameras/dentitions. They cannot verify image modality; use ordinary RGB dental photographs only.
- **Review before movement:** manual 4–12-point outlines, pointer boundary adjustment, keyboard coordinates, optional manual FDI assignment, individual crown review, drag-to-target, rotation and a before/mock-up blend are implemented. Every crown must be reviewed before target movement or image export. Changing a boundary or arch revokes its review and returns the preview to the original. An optional even-row sketch uses only reviewed, arch-assigned crowns from a frontal view and simple bounding geometry; it does not choose a treatment or evaluate clinical feasibility.
- **Evidence and isolation:** the draft response is bound to the SHA-256 of the actual re-encoded AI input. Python, Laravel and the browser check response shape/evidence, coordinate bounds and polygon validity. Empty/failed inference cannot carry accepted findings; the local baseline does not establish image suitability. Late responses are canceled or ignored after photo/view/account changes or intervening crown edits. Invalid/refused/unavailable output never creates fallback detections. Local editing is usable with AI disabled. Guest case data survives the deliberate first sign-in; logout or a change between identified accounts still clears the dental session.
- **Local status:** `GET /api/dental/detector-status` reports runtime `local`, `external_requests:false`, trained/not-trained state, training/validation image counts and a foreground Dice metric when available. `model_capability_verified` remains false. `DENTAL_LOCAL_MODEL_DIR` defaults to `models/dental-local`. A versioned model and its fingerprinted report are loaded afresh, so new training is picked up without restarting. OpenAI credentials have no effect on dental detection or assistance.
- **Advantages/disadvantages:** `contracts/dental/care-options.v1.json` supplies eight draft, source-linked pathways and shared risks. Options include fixed braces, aligners, space assessment, functional appliances during growth, selected specialist jaw-surgery assessment, combined dental/specialist care, retention and clinician-led observation. Each has an overview, potential benefits, tradeoffs and clinician checks. Case associations organize educational discussion; they do not establish patient candidacy, and choosing an option does not calculate the mock-up.
- **Exports:** outline/target JSON and case JSON include photo fingerprints and geometry without pixels/blob URLs. A separate explicit PNG action exports original and mock-up together with an illustration warning and source fingerprint. It requires reviewed outlines and targets within the image; panels are clipped separately and asynchronous exports preserve a single source/state snapshot. The image includes patient pixels because the user explicitly requested that local export. No automatic upload, persistence or model training occurs.

Browser tests use visibly synthetic software-test images and isolated local-service fixtures. They verify actual pixel displacement, local consent, sign-in retention, review gates, training exports, hash checks, stale-response cancellation, benefits/risks content and mobile fit. Python tests perform actual random-forest training/reloading and GrabCut inference on synthetic fixtures in temporary folders. These weights are never installed as the app model. Tests establish software behavior only.

### In-app teaching added 8 November 2026

The local teaching panel now saves reviewed photo/outline examples and starts training directly from the browser. Saving requires authentication, a coded subject/reviewer, a patient partition, complete-annotation attestation and explicit permission to retain the photo locally. Ordinary upload/review still saves nothing. The separate download remains optional. A correction for the same prepared JPEG replaces its prior record; it does not create a duplicate photo. Retain the original upload if further corrections are needed.

Private teaching routes (`GET /api/dental/learning`, `POST /examples`, `DELETE /examples/{hash}`, `POST /train` beneath that path) proxy only to the private Python service. Laravel computes an owner HMAC from the authenticated account and application key; client owner/path fields cannot choose the dataset. Python stores account-separated SQLite datasets under `DENTAL_LEARNING_DIR` (default `training-data/app`), outside public assets and ignored by Git. Photos persist only after the explicit save. Status returns IDs, coded metadata, counts and training stages without photo bytes. These local files are not encrypted; restrict filesystem access to the private workspace.

A single-worker background trainer takes a dataset snapshot, fits the existing colour/texture forest, evaluates separate validation/optional test patients and activates that account's versioned model. The UI shows real stages and engineering metrics, never fabricated percentages or clinical accuracy. A service restart does not resume jobs; use one Python service process. Dataset changes during a run prevent activation. Removing/updating an example invalidates saved model versions, and deleted SQLite content is cleared/vacuumed. Other accounts cannot use the model or inspect/remove the teaching examples. The teaching set can be managed after reload without attaching another photograph.

Python tests exercise actual training, model activation, separate-owner inference, persistence, corrected-label replacement, removal and concurrent dataset changes using temporary synthetic fixtures. PHP feature tests verify authentication, permissions, owner derivation, request/response filtering and protected progress/deletion. Browser fixtures cover save permissions, training consent, automatic progress polling, model availability refresh, reload, removal and mobile layout. No synthetic fixture weights are installed as the user's model. No private app patient examples have been supplied or trained during implementation; the separate public-data run is described above.

### Local machine learning added 7 November 2026

The **Teach your local model** panel exports a separate `humantwin-local-crowns-v1` JSON example containing a re-encoded RGB photo, SHA-256, source crown polygons, coded subject/reviewer IDs, explicit training permission, complete-annotation attestation and patient partition. All crowns must be reviewed. Unmarked pixels become negative/background labels, so incomplete annotations are unsuitable. Moving photo targets does not become training supervision.

The app now trains with its button. For separate offline research, run `powershell -File scripts/train-dental-local.ps1` after saving exports in `ai-service/training-data`, or use `ai-service/scripts/train-dental-local.py`. Offline operator models are separate from account-specific app models. The CLI requires at least two training subjects and one different validation subject. This is only a pipeline minimum, not a sufficient medical dataset. It rejects conflicting subject partitions, duplicate images, near-duplicates crossing partitions, incorrect photo fingerprints, overlapping crowns and missing foreground/background. Split codes and perceptual checks cannot prove patient independence; curators must verify it. Up to 200 examples are supported by this bounded baseline trainer.

The trainer fits a three-class random forest on training pixels only. Validation and optional untouched test images produce aggregate foreground Dice, which does not measure per-crown identity or clinical performance. Failed training preserves the previous model. Successful training writes an immutable XML model and a count/metric report, then atomically changes the active version. No model download, API key, paid service, web scraping or network training is involved. Models and datasets are ignored by Git; the model folder does not contain source images or subject/reviewer codes. Models should still be treated as private derived artifacts. Correcting an outline alone does not retrain the saved model.

Primary implementation references: [OpenCV random trees](https://docs.opencv.org/4.10.0/d0/d65/classcv_1_1ml_1_1RTrees.html) and [OpenCV GrabCut](https://docs.opencv.org/4.x/d8/d83/tutorial_py_grabcut.html). This small colour/texture baseline is not a deep neural network and is not clinically validated.

### Case room added 7 November 2026

Open `/#dental-cases` or **Cases & practice**. The existing braces view also links to this room.

- **Patient photo review:** up to six local JPEG/PNG/WebP photos, actual file signature and decode checks, 10 MB / 24-million-pixel limits, duplicate detection, view labels, selected-photo review and manual FDI tooth markers. Keyboard coordinate controls accompany click placement. Photos and free text stay in memory. Reload, clear case or account change clears them; removed and unmounted object URLs are revoked.
- **26 educational topics / 20 references:** `contracts/dental/orthodontics.v1.json` contains original short summaries, recognition prompts, clinician review considerations, source IDs, explicit limitations and four-option questions. Sources include AAO, BOS, NHS trusts, AAPD and university publications. These are draft authored educational records, not expert-labeled patient cases or a trained dataset. Crowding, generalized spacing, diastema, deep bite, anterior/posterior open bites, reverse/increased overjet, anterior/posterior/scissor crossbites, Class II divisions and Class III dental relationships, midline discrepancies, rotations, canine impaction, absent/extra teeth, ectopic eruption, mixed dentition, relapse, wear, gingival display and asymmetry are included.
- **Interactive teaching model:** 21 topics have deliberately exaggerated, dimensionless tooth or jaw offsets. Other topics use a clearly labeled reference arch and clinical vignette. Intensity and illustrative alignment controls, per-tooth position/rotation edits, FDI selection, four camera views and existing bracket options are available. Uploaded photos never become this geometry. Missing teeth are not restored by the alignment slider. There are no measured patient movements, forces, bone/root boundaries or predicted treatment times.
- **Student practice:** ten randomized questions, locked submission, per-question reasoning and reference links, a final score, missed-topic links and result export. A separately reviewed local case can also become a photograph exercise: case-level discussion labels are hidden until submission, then compared with the student's selections. Self-attested labels may be incomplete or incorrect; agreement is not diagnostic accuracy. Practice does not establish clinical competence. The audience selector changes wording only; it is not verification of professional status or authorization.
- **Case / research exports:** case notes and user-selected labels export as JSON. A reviewer code, photo, selected labels and self-attestation are required for research JSONL export. Exports include SHA-256 photo fingerprints and manual markers, never image bytes or blob URLs. The proposed partition groups repeated coded patient IDs; dataset curators still need verified ownership, independent label review, dataset-wide duplicate checks, meaningful split design and external evaluation. Exporting does not train, fine-tune or validate any model.
- **Local study coach:** searches the versioned source library in the browser. The earlier external review endpoints now return an explicit unavailable response without forwarding any content. Provider configuration for other body modules cannot enable external dental review.

Local outline requests require app authentication and request-specific local-processing consent. The selected photo is resized to at most 1024 pixels and re-encoded as JPEG without its original metadata. Source photo fingerprints bind every response. No image is saved by inference. Model training is a separate explicit local command and never starts from an ordinary upload.

### Existing atlas and research tools

- Original 3D atlas of 32 permanent teeth, using FDI notation from the patient's perspective. Select by mesh picking, numbered buttons or arrow-key chart navigation. Upper/lower, front, side and tooth-section views have orbit, pan, zoom and reset controls.
- Different incisor, canine, premolar and molar teaching geometry, enamel/dentin/pulp/root sections, gingiva and source-linked learning topics. Geometry is dimensionless and illustrative; morphology, root counts and eruption vary between people.
- Metal and ceramic bracket visualizations, ligature colors, archwires and a jaw-separation display control. No orthodontic forces, treatment stages, aligner schedule, movement prediction or appliance prescription is calculated.
- Separate pain records for each selected tooth, explicit symptom and appliance fields, a safety-sign screen, source-linked next steps and JSON summary export. No diagnosis or probability is generated.
- Local STL, OBJ and self-contained GLB review, including paired upper/lower surfaces. The pair shares a single centering/scaling transform. Original units are normalized for display; no physical measurements or tooth segmentation are inferred from a surface. External GLB resource URLs are rejected.
- Local PNG/JPEG/WebP image review, a published cephalogram with its 29 expert labels, manual two-point distances and three-point angles, keyboard coordinate fields, calibration entry and manual-point JSON export. Millimetres require a documented mm/pixel scale supplied by the reviewer. There is no trained landmark detector or radiographic interpretation.
- CSV/JSON measurement imports with coded subjects, chronological charts, first-to-last observed change, original-file export and SHA-256 fingerprint. Streams are separated by subject, tooth, metric and unit. Blank numbers, invalid dates, malformed CSV/JSON, duplicate observations and mixed units are rejected. Excel sheets must first be exported as CSV. There is no automatic correction, interpolation of missing observations or forecast.

## Public research data actually fetched

The files are in `frontend/public/data/dental/`. `manifest.json` records each original filename, author/source, release, byte count, modification status and SHA-256 fingerprint. `ATTRIBUTION.md` supplies attribution and license links.

1. **Serial 3D Dental Models**, Abdi and Nouri (2020), [Mendeley Data V1](https://doi.org/10.17632/bpnmf2vhsk.1), **CC BY 4.0**: two unchanged STL casts from the same published visit, one upper and one lower. The full source is longitudinal data from 24 participants with normal occlusion and no previous orthodontic treatment. This is not a treatment-response dataset.
2. **Aariz Cephalometric Dataset**, Khalid et al. (2025), [Figshare V1](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.27986417.v1), **CC BY 4.0 dataset license**: one unchanged validation JPEG and its corresponding senior orthodontist annotation JSON. The full release has 1,000 radiographs and 29 expert landmarks. The ~2.1 GB ZIP was not downloaded in full: the reproducible fetcher reads its ZIP directory and selected original entries with bounded HTTP byte ranges. The journal article's separate license is not used to license the dataset.
3. **Orthodont**, [R nlme / CRAN R-universe](https://cran.r-universe.dev/nlme), **package distribution GPL ≥ 2**, original data Potthoff and Roy (1964), UNC Dental School: all 108 unchanged CSV observations from 27 coded subjects at ages 8, 10, 12 and 14. These are pituitary-to-pterygomaxillary-fissure distances in millimetres, not tooth movement, overjet, brace outcomes or population-normal thresholds. The full GPL-2 license text is included.

These are independent references, not three modalities from one matched patient. Public record identifiers are retained for reproducibility. Local research imports should be appropriately de-identified and used under the researcher's permissions.

The source library also links the official **Teeth3DS+** release and **Bite2Text** access page. Their full datasets have not been bundled. Teeth3DS+ website licensing is not assumed to license every OSF file. Bite2Text requires registration and release terms; no account was created and no access controls were bypassed.

Reproduce the reference downloads:

```powershell
cd frontend
npm run data:dental
```

## Measurement import contract

CSV headers or JSON object fields:

```csv
subject_id,recorded_at,tooth_fdi,metric,value,unit
STUDY_01,2026-10-01,11,pain,4,score
STUDY_01,2026-10-15,11,pain,2,score
```

Use coded IDs of 1–48 letters/numbers/underscores/hyphens, `YYYY-MM-DD` or a UTC ISO timestamp, a permanent FDI tooth or blank, a lowercase metric identifier, a finite number and `mm`, `degrees`, `score` or `hours`. The importer accepts up to 10,000 rows / 2 MB. File size and numerical bounds are software resource/input checks, not clinical normal ranges. R's original `distance,age,Subject,Sex` format is recognized separately, without fabricating dates or associating skull distances with a tooth.

## Safety, privacy and validation boundary

Guidance is deterministic, versioned source-based software requiring dental review. Primary sources: [NHS toothache](https://www.nhs.uk/symptoms/toothache/), [NHS abscess](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/dental-abscess/), [NHS Fife orthodontic emergencies](https://www.nhsfife.org/services/all-services/patient-advice/orthodontic-emergencies/), [AAO emergencies](https://aaoinfo.org/whats-trending/what-is-an-orthodontic-emergency/), [ADA tooth anatomy](https://www.mouthhealthy.org/all-topics-a-z/tooth/) and [NHS braces](https://www.nhs.uk/tests-and-treatments/braces/). Links and draft content were checked on 5 November 2026. References are not endorsements or validation of this implementation.

Emergency signs take precedence before screen completion. A knocked-out or severely displaced permanent tooth receives immediate dental guidance. Emergency advice remains visible after edits, tooth changes and mode switches until an explicit new pain assessment. A score of 7/10 is an **unvalidated conservative software escalation flag**, not a published diagnostic cutoff. An unchecked safety review is incomplete, never interpreted as a negative screen. Medication doses, antibiotics, DIY appliance adjustments and individualized treatment recommendations are not provided.

New pain assessment clears the tooth-specific pain session. Research data and case photos have separate clear/close controls. Data stays in component memory and is cleared on reload or account change; image object URLs and GPU resources are released on unmount. Dental outline requests use only the private local service. There is no automatic dental upload, photo storage or training. Explicit in-app teaching saves retain permitted photos in the private account-scoped local dataset; explicit training exports also contain actual photos. Training saves a model and aggregate report separately, without copying raw photos. Pre-existing eye and general assessment APIs remain independent.

Before clinical use: dental/orthodontic review of the numbering, geometry and care pathways; locale/age-specific validation; clinician-defined measurement protocols and calibration; subgroup and failure evaluation; consent and secure record design; and an applicable regulatory assessment. Real tooth movement cannot be inferred from the bundled historical growth table or the appliance display.
